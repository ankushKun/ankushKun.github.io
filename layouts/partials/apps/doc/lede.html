{{- /* Decides what the header lede says and what the body starts with, so a page never shows the same sentence twice (summary in the header, then again as the opening paragraph). Returns a dict: lede safe HTML for .doc-lede, or "" for no lede (always plain weight: bold/italics are stripped, links are kept) content the page's .Content, minus the opening paragraph when it moved up into the lede Rules - The lede comes from `summary` (or `description`), as plain text. - A summary that only repeats the title (80% of its words are in it) is not shown. - Timeline entries: bodies are a few lines and the summary is usually a polished rewrite of them, so the lede is dropped when the body starts with it or when at least half of its words appear in the title and body together ("Started college as a CS major." over "CS Major"). ("Words" here are 3+ letters and not filler such as "the", "with", "built".) The summary still feeds list views and the meta description. - Projects and blog posts: when the body OPENS with a paragraph that restates the summary (it contains 55% or more of the summary's words), that paragraph becomes the lede, in the lede's place and style, and is left out of the body. Its words are kept, only moved. An opening longer than about two sentences (320 characters) stays in the body and the header simply has no lede. The markdown and feed outputs are unaffected (they print the full body). Usage: {{ $ld := partial "apps/doc/lede.html" (dict "page" . "kind" "project") }} */ -}} {{- $p := .page -}} {{- $kind := .kind -}} {{- $content := string $p.Content -}} {{- $ledeHTML := "" -}} {{- $lede := "" -}} {{- with or $p.Params.summary $p.Description -}} {{- $lede = . | markdownify | plainify | htmlUnescape | strings.TrimSpace -}} {{- end -}} {{- if $lede -}} {{- $ledeHTML = $lede | htmlEscape -}} {{- $norm := `[^a-z0-9]+` -}} {{- $ledeKey := $lede | lower | replaceRE $norm "" -}} {{- $filler := slice "the" "and" "for" "with" "that" "are" "was" "from" "into" "its" "this" "you" "your" "our" "their" "can" "has" "have" "but" "not" "all" "any" "via" "using" "use" "built" -}} {{- $ledeWords := complement $filler (findRE `[a-z0-9]{3,}` (lower $lede) | uniq) -}} {{- $titleWords := findRE `[a-z0-9]+` (lower $p.Title) | uniq -}} {{- if and $ledeWords (ge (mul (len (intersect $ledeWords $titleWords)) 5) (mul (len $ledeWords) 4)) -}} {{- /* The summary only repeats the title ("Graduated high school (12th standard)." under "Graduated High School [12th Standard]") */ -}} {{- $ledeHTML = "" -}} {{- else if eq $kind "timeline" -}} {{- $bodyText := $p.Plain | htmlUnescape | lower -}} {{- $bodyKey := $bodyText | replaceRE $norm "" -}} {{- if and $ledeKey (strings.HasPrefix $bodyKey $ledeKey) -}} {{- $ledeHTML = "" -}} {{- else if and $bodyKey $ledeWords -}} {{- $shared := intersect $ledeWords (union $titleWords (findRE `[a-z0-9]+` $bodyText | uniq)) -}} {{- if ge (mul (len $shared) 2) (len $ledeWords) }}{{ $ledeHTML = "" }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- else if $ledeWords -}} {{- with findRESubmatch `^\s*

((?s:.*?))

` $content 1 -}} {{- $first := index (index . 0) 1 -}} {{- $firstText := $first | plainify | htmlUnescape | lower -}} {{- $shared := intersect $ledeWords (findRE `[a-z0-9]+` $firstText | uniq) -}} {{- if ge (mul (len $shared) 20) (mul (len $ledeWords) 11) -}} {{- if le (len $firstText) 320 -}} {{- $ledeHTML = $first | replaceRE `` "" | replaceRE `\s*\s*` " " | strings.TrimSpace -}} {{- $content = replaceRE `^\s*

(?s:.*?)

\s*` "" $content 1 -}} {{- else -}} {{- /* Too long to read as a lede: the body keeps it, the summary goes */ -}} {{- $ledeHTML = "" -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return dict "lede" ($ledeHTML | safeHTML) "content" ($content | safeHTML) -}}
