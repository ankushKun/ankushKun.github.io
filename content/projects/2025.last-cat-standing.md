**You are the last survivor of your kind. Can you withstand the dangers of the island?**

A survival RPG built for the web, where you explore, fight, and survive as a lone cat in a hostile world.

## 🎮 Gameplay Summary
- **Explore:** Traverse a mysterious island filled with secrets.
- **Survive:** Manage resources and avoid dangers.
- **Fight:** Combat enemies using unique cat-like abilities.

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Platform:** WebGL (Itch.io).
