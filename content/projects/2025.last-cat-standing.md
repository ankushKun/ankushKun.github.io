**You are the last survivor of your kind. Or so you thought.**

I made it during the Arweave India Stealth House in Pondicherry and put it up on itch.io on 26 September 2025. It's built in Unity and runs in the browser through WebGL.

## 🎮 Gameplay Summary
- **Survive:** You're hungry and scared of the water, so you live on the apples you find along the way.
- **Climb:** Get past the obstacles and find your way to the top of a cliff, hoping there's fish up there.
- **Race:** Be the fastest cat, or grab as many apples as you can on the way up.

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Engine:** Unity, exported to WebGL and hosted on Itch.io.
