## 🌍 What is pixels.earth?
- A **massive, collaborative canvas** overlaid on a map of Earth
- Draw **anywhere in the world** — your local neighborhood, famous landmarks, or unexplored territories
- Every pixel is an **on-chain transaction** on Solana
- Built on **MagicBlock** for real-time, sub-second blockchain interactions

## 🏠 Land Ownership (Shards)
- The world is divided into **shards** — regions you can unlock and own
- Unlock a shard to **claim ownership** of that piece of the canvas
- Earn from activity on shards you own
- A new model for **decentralized digital land ownership**

## ⚡ Tech Stack
- **Solana** for fast, low-cost on-chain transactions
- **Anchor** for solana rust programs
- **MagicBlock** for real-time multiplayer interactions and sub-millisecond latency
- Progressive Web App (PWA) — works on desktop and mobile

## 🎯 Why It Matters
- Combines the viral appeal of collaborative pixel art (like r/place) with **real-world geography**
- A live experiment in **high-throughput blockchain gaming**
- Shows what's possible when you build real-time multiplayer apps on Solana
