**BetterIDEa is a developer tooling suite for the Arweave/AO ecosystem that began as a hackathon project—and grew into a key part of the ecosystem, ultimately being acquired by Forward Research.**

## 🏆 Hackathon Origins
- Built during **Arweave India’s first hackerhouse**
- Won **1st place** for its initial MVP: a simple, web-based IDE for Arweave
- Post hackathon, it expanded beyond basic editing into a full development environment  

## 🌍 Global Debut
- **Made its public debut alongside the global launch of AO**  
- Featured during the official AO launch event: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9snJSH9CdUs

## 🧩 Evolving Into a Dev Suite
- Introduced **npm packages, CLI tools, and integrations** for a seamless developer experience  
- Refined into a cleaner and more intuitive interface for decentralized development

## 📈 Impact & Adoption
- Adopted by builders entering the Arweave/AO ecosystem  
- Simplified the workflow of developing on permanent storage and compute

## 🤝 Acquisition
- **Acquired by Forward Research**, the core team behind AO  
- I joined as an **Entrepreneur in Residence** to continue advancing developer tooling in the ecosystem
