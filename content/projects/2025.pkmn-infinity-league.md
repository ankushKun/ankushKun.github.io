After beating Red on top of Mt. Silver it gets colder than it has ever been. Your Pokemon faint one by one, there's no Escape Rope in your bag, and you pass out in the snow. You wake up in a strange realm with twisted rules, where you don't fight for badges anymore, you fight to survive, and every win gets you a little closer to escaping.

I built it in Godot for the GMTK Game Jam 2025, where the theme was Loop, so the whole game is one endless run of battles. I submitted it on 1 August and kept pushing builds until 3 August (V1, V3 and V4 are up on the GitHub releases). It got disqualified for using Pokemon assets I don't have the rights to. Oh boy, rip.
