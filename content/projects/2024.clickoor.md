**A point-and-shoot camera that never runs out of film.**

Clickoor automatically uploads every photo to the Arweave Permaweb, ensuring your memories are stored forever on-chain without any configuration.

## 📸 Features
- **Infinite Storage:** Powered by Arweave's permanent web.
- **Zero Config:** Just point and shoot.
- **On-Chain:** Photos are immutable and censorship-resistant.

## 🛠️ Hardware/Software
- **Web App:** Runs on mobile devices acting as the camera interface.
- **Arweave:** Handles the decentralized storage backend.
