**sh23ds is Silent Hill 2 on a Nintendo 3DS, running as native homebrew.**

It isn't an emulator. The game's camera, lighting, fog, events, enemies and cutscenes were ported function by function from the PC Director's Cut into a new engine in C for the 3DS's ARM11 CPU and PICA200 GPU. A packer turns your own PC copy into the 3DS's formats, in the browser or with Docker, and the app installs as a CIA with FBI.

The whole game is in: all 17 areas, every puzzle and boss, the six endings, New Game+ and Born From a Wish, with the map and items on the touch screen. The New 3DS gets full settings and the original 3DS and 2DS get a lighter 30 fps profile.

## Features
- Every area, cutscene and ending of the 2001 game, plus Born From a Wish
- The PC's fog layer, room lighting and James' flashlight as a per-pixel spotlight
- The Silent Hill 2: Enhanced Edition fixes built in, and its packs used if you have them
- Music and sound from the game's own archives, with the music decoded on the second core
- A browser packer (Pyodide and ffmpeg.wasm) that never uploads your files

## Built With
- C with libctru and citro3d, built with devkitARM in Docker
- Python converters for every PC format, shared by the browser and Docker packers
- Ghidra, with names from the PS2 beta's debug information and the Enhanced Edition source

Version 0.1.2 is a development build. The repository and releases contain no game files; you need the PC release of the Director's Cut. GPLv3, and not affiliated with Konami or Nintendo.
