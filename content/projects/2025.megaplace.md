**Earth is your canvas. A high-speed, on-chain evolution of Reddit's r/place.**

MegaPlace leverages **MegaETH's** sub-millisecond latency to create a real-time multiplayer experience where every pixel placed is a permanent on-chain transaction.

## 🎨 The Concept
- **Collaborative Art:** Users work together (or fight) to draw on a shared canvas.
- **On-Chain:** Unlike r/place, the state is stored on a blockchain, making it censorship-resistant and permanent.
- **Performance:** Demonstrates the power of MegaETH by handling high-frequency transactions in real-time.
- **No popups:** session keys let you place pixels without approving every transaction
- **Premium mode:** 0.01 ETH unlocks two hours without rate limits

## ⚡ Tech Stack
- **Solidity** contracts (Hardhat + OpenZeppelin) on MegaETH testnet
- **Bun + Express + Viem** backend that caches the canvas and pushes live updates over SSE
- **React + Wagmi + Leaflet** frontend, with the canvas mapped onto the globe in Web Mercator
