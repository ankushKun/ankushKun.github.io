Right now I'm building my startup - [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer). It's an AI workforce where the agents get their own cloud computers, so they can actually finish work instead of just talking about it. Think browser, terminal, files, memory, the whole desk - not another chat window.

That's the latest chapter. Before this I was deep in developer tools, on-chain infra, communities, and a ridiculous number of hackathons. I like building things that ship, breaking stuff for fun, and being the kind of nerd who will stay up all night to make something cool work.

I build developer tools that scale. As an **Entrepreneur in Residence at Forward Research**, I shipped 5+ production-ready developer tools used by 1K+ developers across the ecosystem — including [BetterIDEa](https://betteridea.arweave.net) (acquired), [AO Package Manager](https://apm_betteridea.arweave.net) (now part of AO core infra), and [Subspace](https://subspace.arweave.net).

At **Arweave India**, I provided technical mentorship to 100+ developers, helping teams ship on-chain projects, and co-organized 4+ hacker houses to grow the builder community.

Earlier, at **Physics Wallah**, I built an interactive live-class system that processed 10K+ real-time chat messages for classes with 50K+ concurrent viewers.

My journey into tech started long before blockchain. I've always loved experimenting — from building robots and mini-satellites to making objects levitate with sound waves.

Since 2017, I've competed in 50+ hackathons across ecosystems like Ethereum, Solana, Arweave, and more — earning more than a dozen prizes. One of my favorite projects evolved into [Arlink](https://arlink.arweave.net) — Arweave's flagship deployment tool that raised a seed round at $2.5M valuation.

In 2023, I won the first Arweave India Hacker House. The story behind it is something I share in my ["TEDx" talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWW4BCZPIUg).

Outside of coding, I love sharing ideas, experiments, and live builds on my [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@AnkushKun). You'll find everything from developer explainers to fun physics demos.
