Construct made it into the Build Sprint of the [TinyFish Accelerator](https://www.tinyfish.ai/accelerator), a program for founders building AI agents that work on the live web. The sprint was a two week virtual intensive from 31 March to 14 April, which happened to be right when we were moving Construct's backend off a VPS and onto Cloudflare.

You can find Construct on TinyFish's founders page along with the other teams that took part.
