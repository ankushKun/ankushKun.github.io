Less than a month after Construct's launch I was back on Product Hunt, this time with NepoNet, built by the same team. Every intro that helped Construct's launch came through a friend of a friend, and finding those chains by hand took weeks, so we built a tool that does it for you.

[NepoNet]({{< ref "/projects/2026.neponet.md" >}}) finds the warmest chain of intros from you to anyone on X. Vibhansh designed it and I built it. The MCP server and bring-your-own-AI plans landed in the days before launch, and the day after, the API moved onto our own server because the follow graph had outgrown its Durable Object. It finished #9 Product of the Day. If you are planning a launch of your own, here is [how to promote a Product Hunt launch]({{< ref "/blogs/where-to-promote-product-hunt-launch.md" >}}).
