Co-founder of [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer), which I started with Nischal Naik in February 2026.

It's a workforce of AI employees that each get their own cloud computer, so they can actually finish work instead of just talking about it. Think browser, terminal, files, memory, the whole desk, not another chat window. The first desktop build was running a week later and the waitlist opened at the start of March.
