Grok Bot is xAI's AI teammate with a computer of its own, out of beta since Grok Bot 1.0 on November 2, 2026. New buyers get it only through The X Subscription, from Premium at $30 a month ($300 a year); Plus at $8 doesn't include it. Its usage comes out of a weekly pool shared with Grok and Cursor, which xAI tightened over Halloween weekend. The right alternative depends on why you're looking: the same kind of agent from another lab (ChatGPT dots, Claude), Grok's models inside a different agent (Hermes Agent, OpenClaw, Construct), agents a whole team shares in its chat apps (Construct, Lindy, Viktor), or an agent you run yourself (OpenClaw, Hermes Agent).

We make Construct, one of the options below. Alternatives are grouped by the job they do, not ranked, and every outside fact links to where we read it.

## How we checked this

On November 13, 2026, we read xAI's Grok Bot docs, its X Subscription pricing page and launch posts, and each alternative's own pricing and product pages. For news we used TechCrunch. This is a review of public pages, not a hands-on test of output quality. Where xAI's own pages disagree, we give both figures. Prices are in US dollars. Our [editorial policy](https://construct.computer/editorial-policy/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=crosspost) covers how we write comparisons.

## What is Grok Bot now?

xAI's docs call Grok Bot "your AI teammate with a computer" ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/how-grok-bot-works), checked Nov 2026). Every Bot on an account works on "one persistent cloud computer" with a browser, command line and filesystem ([xAI FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)). You can ask a Bot to set up routines on a schedule or an event, up to 50 per Bot, and teach it a skill by recording a browser workflow for up to ten minutes ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/skills-routines-and-automations)). Auto Review checks each action before the Bot takes it, with up to 20 Allow rules and 20 Ask first rules ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/approvals-security-and-privacy)).

Grok Bot 1.0 on November 2 added Team Bots you can bring into Slack, Microsoft Teams and video calls, and Bots that "recognize work that needs to be done and offer to take it on" ([xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0)). You also reach it in X Chat on iOS, Android and x.com, and by phone in the US only ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/chat-and-collaboration)). Each user can claim one email address at mail.grokbot.com.

## How much does Grok Bot cost?

Grok Bot has no price of its own. "Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super, and Ultra", and "Plus does not include Grok Bot" ([xAI pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), checked Nov 13, 2026).

| The X Subscription | Monthly | Yearly | Grok Bot | Included usage |
| ------------------ | ------- | ------ | -------- | -------------- |
| Free               | $0      | $0     | No       | Limited        |
| Plus               | $8      | $80    | No       | Base           |
| Premium            | $30     | $300   | Yes      | 4x             |
| Super              | $100    | $1,000 | Yes      | 15x            |
| Ultra              | $200    | $2,000 | Yes      | 50x            |

Teams pay the same per seat: Premium $30, Super $100 and Ultra $200, and Plus isn't offered to teams. Existing Cursor, SuperGrok and X Premium plans carry on unless you change them ([xAI pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing)). Some accounts get a trial, which is "a usage credit" with a 7-day window, and "It never turns into a paid plan" ([xAI FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)).

## What are Grok Bot's limits?

**A weekly pool you share with Grok and Cursor.** Bot usage "draws from that seat's shared weekly usage pool, not a separate additive pool", the same pool that covers Grok models in Grok and Cursor ([xAI pricing](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing)). xAI's pages disagree on the size of the tiers: the docs table puts Super at 15x and Ultra at 50x against Plus, which works out to 3.75x and 12.5x Premium, while the [Grok Bot page](https://x.ai/bot) says 3.5x and 12x Premium. Top-ups buy Additional Usage credits, at a price the page doesn't list.

**Limits tightened in November.** Over Halloween weekend, some subscribers found their weekly usage maxed out without using the Bot overnight, and Elon Musk confirmed it was not a glitch: "The cost of serving Grok Bot was unsustainably high relative to revenue" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/02/elon-musk-throttles-grok-bot-limits-due-to-unsustainably-high-costs/), Nov 2, 2026). On a legacy plan with on-demand usage off, "Grok Bot stops when weekly usage runs out and resumes when weekly usage resets" ([xAI FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)).

**No model choice.** "Grok Bot picks the model for each task, and there is no model picker" ([xAI FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)). The same FAQ mentions tasks routed to Opus 5.5, a Claude model, so the work isn't always done by Grok.

**One computer for every Bot.** Because Bots share a computer, xAI says "Do not use separate Bots as a security boundary" ([xAI FAQ](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/faq)). Grok Bot also "requires cloud data storage, so Legacy Privacy Mode is not supported", and its computer "uses Cursor's shared network, which some sites block" ([xAI troubleshooting](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/troubleshooting)).

### The same kind of agent from another lab

**ChatGPT dots** are OpenAI's always-on agents, each with "its own computer and browser". They come with ChatGPT Pro ($100, $200 or $500 a month) and Business Premium, and Pro access excludes the EEA, the UK and Switzerland ([OpenAI](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots.md), checked Nov 2026). A dot draws on different models for different parts of its work, and OpenAI describes no way to choose them.

**Claude** now does everything Cowork did from any conversation, on every paid plan from Pro at $20 a month ([Claude](https://claude.com/product/cowork), [Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), checked Nov 2026). Its scheduled tasks run in the cloud with your computer asleep, and you keep the model picker. Claude Duet, Anthropic's proactive agent, is a US-only research preview with a waitlist ([Claude](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet)).

Our [comparison of always-on agents](https://construct.computer/blog/always-on-ai-agents-compared/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=crosspost) covers these, Tasks in Gemini, Muse, Copilot Cowork and Perplexity in more detail.

### Grok's models in a different agent

If you like Grok but not the Bot, you can bring Grok into another agent.

**Hermes Agent**, from Nous Research, is free and MIT licensed and runs on your machine or in Nous's cloud ([Nous Research](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/open-source-ai-agent), checked Nov 2026). Since May 15, 2026, Grok subscribers can sign in with their SuperGrok subscription and use Grok inside it ([xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-hermes)). It talks over Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp and email.

**OpenClaw** is a free, MIT-licensed agent you host yourself, using "an API key from your chosen provider" ([OpenClaw docs](https://docs.openclaw.ai/)).

**Construct** (ours) can run its agents on Grok with your own xAI API key on Starter ($49 a month) and Pro ($259). You choose whether your plan's usage or your key goes first ([pricing](https://construct.computer/pricing/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=crosspost)). Unlike Grok Bot, you pick the model, and each agent can use a different one.

### Agents a team shares in its chat apps

Grok Bot's Team Bots need a Team or Enterprise plan ([xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-1-0)), and every member needs a seat. Three alternatives price this differently.

**Construct** (ours) is a crew of agents, each with its own computer and browser, that you can mention or message in Discord, Telegram and Slack. A team has its own plan covering everyone in it, with owner, admin and member roles: Lite is $9 a month, Starter $49 and Pro $259, and every new account gets free usage once to try it ([pricing](https://construct.computer/pricing/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=crosspost)). Agents ask before anything goes out or changes.

**Lindy** lives in Slack, with a private assistant per person and a shared one in channels, from $49.99 a month per active user ([Lindy pricing](https://www.lindy.ai/pricing), checked Nov 2026).

**Viktor** lives in Slack and Microsoft Teams, priced per workspace rather than per seat, from $50 a month for 20,000 credits, with free credits to start ([Viktor pricing](https://viktor.com/pricing), checked Nov 2026).

### An agent you run yourself

**OpenClaw** and **Hermes Agent** are both free and open source. OpenClaw talks over WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Signal, iMessage and more, and runs cron jobs ([OpenClaw docs](https://docs.openclaw.ai/automation)). Its security model assumes "one trusted boundary per gateway" ([OpenClaw security](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/security)), so a shared machine still needs care. Hermes Agent has paid Nous Portal plans from $20 to $200 a month if you want hosted tools and a cloud agent ([Nous Research](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/open-source-ai-agent)). Running either means updates, keys and uptime are yours to look after; our page on [building your own agent](https://construct.computer/blog/construct-vs-diy/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=crosspost) covers that tradeoff.

## When Grok Bot is the better pick

- You already pay for Premium or above, a Cursor plan or an eligible SuperGrok plan, so the Bot costs you nothing extra.
- You code in Cursor and want one usage pool across Grok, Cursor and the Bot.
- You'd rather teach a routine by showing it in the browser than describe it in words.
- You want the agent in X Chat, or on a phone call in the US.
- Your company already manages Cursor with SSO and SCIM, which carry over to Grok Bot's team admin ([xAI docs](https://docs.x.ai/grok-bot/teams-and-enterprises)).

If the reason you're looking is the weekly limit, check whether the alternative has one too. Claude has a five-hour and a weekly limit ([Claude help](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11647753-how-do-usage-and-length-limits-work)), Lindy pauses at its credit limit, and Construct has a weekly limit inside each month's usage. A self-hosted agent on your own model key is the only option here whose limit is set by your own spending.
