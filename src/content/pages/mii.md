---
title: Mii
description: Load a Wii U or 3DS Mii from its QR code and get the code that puts it on the plaza.
---

Open, paste or drop a Mii QR code from a Wii U, 3DS or Miitomo. To add Miis to the plaza, put their QR images in `src/data/miis/`, or paste a code into `ME` or `HERO` in `src/data/plaza.ts`.
