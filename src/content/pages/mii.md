---
title: Mii Studio
description: Inspect any Wii U or 3DS Mii in 3D, decode Mii QR codes, or shuffle through the plaza crowd.
---

Drop, paste, or upload any Wii U, 3DS, or Miitomo QR code to spin it around in real FFL 3D and grab its Mii code, or hit **Random Plaza Mii** to inspect one of the familiar faces hanging out on the home page.
