## Built with

- [Astro](https://astro.build/): static site generation and content collections
- [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) and `@tailwindcss/typography`: base layout and prose styling
- [three.js](https://threejs.org/): powers the 3D WaraWara Plaza and Mii Studio stages
- [FFL.js](https://github.com/ariankordi/FFL.js) by ariankordi, through the [Mii Creator](https://github.com/ankushKun/mii-creator) fork: renders genuine Wii U Mii heads via WebAssembly
- [GUN](https://gun.eco/): decentralized real-time sync behind the Miiverse chat room
- [jsQR](https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR): decodes Wii U / 3DS Mii QR codes in the browser
- [Hugeicons](https://hugeicons.com/) (`@hugeicons/core-free-icons`): the TV's buttons and the glyphs in link buttons
- [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/) (`simple-icons`, CC0): the real platform marks in link buttons; any other site's button shows that site's own icon
- [M PLUS 1p](https://mplusfonts.github.io/), [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/), and [Monocraft](https://github.com/IdreesInc/Monocraft) (SIL OFL): typography for the TV UI, code blocks, social cards, and Plaza name tags
- [Satori](https://github.com/vercel/satori) and [sharp](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/): build-time image processing and Open Graph card generation

## Content & artwork

- Articles cross-posted from [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer/) retain their original charts and cover artwork.
- Screenshots, banners, and logos across project channels come from each respective project and release.
- Mii, the Wii U Mii bodies, the FFL face resource, and the plaza music belong to Nintendo.
- The famous faces in the plaza crowd are fan-made Miis shared on [MiiCharacters.com](https://www.miicharacters.com/).
- All trademarks, game titles, and product names mentioned across blog posts and project pages belong to their respective owners.
