## Built with

- [Astro](https://astro.build/) — static site generation and content collections
- [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) and `@tailwindcss/typography` — base layout and prose styling
- [Satori](https://github.com/vercel/satori) and [sharp](https://sharp.pixelplumbing.com/) — build-time thumbnail and Open Graph card generation
- [Inter](https://rsms.me/inter/) and [JetBrains Mono](https://www.jetbrains.com/lp/mono/) (via [Fontsource](https://fontsource.org/)) — bundled open-source fonts for generated social cards

## Content & artwork

- Articles cross-posted from [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer/) retain their original charts and cover artwork.
- Screenshots and artwork for `3dsDL`, `3dsFM`, `sh23ds`, `Animedoro`, and `oof` are from those projects.
- All trademarks, game titles, and product names mentioned across blog posts and project pages belong to their respective owners.
