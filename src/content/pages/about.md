Right now I'm building [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=about) with Nischal Naik. It's a workforce of AI employees where each agent gets its own cloud computer — browser, terminal, files, email, and memory — so it can finish work instead of just talking about it. We launched on Product Hunt in August 2026 and finished #1 Product of the Day.

Before Construct, I spent most of my time in developer tooling, distributed systems, communities, and way too many hackathons. I like building things that ship, taking systems apart to see how they work, and staying up too late trying to make weird hardware or game ports behave.

## Background

As an **Entrepreneur in Residence at Forward Research**, I shipped developer tools used by 1,000+ builders across the Arweave and AO ecosystem — including [BetterIDEa](/projects/betteridea/) (which Forward Research acquired), the [AO Package Manager](/projects/apm/) (now loaded by default in every `aos` process), [WAuth](/projects/wauth/), and [Subspace](/projects/subspace/).

At **Arweave India**, I mentored 100+ developers, judged 40+ builds, and co-organized hacker houses and [Arweave Day India](/talks/arweave-day-india-2025/).

Earlier, as a **Software Engineer Intern at Physics Wallah**, I built a real-time live-class tool that parsed 10K+ YouTube chat messages for classes with 50K+ concurrent viewers.

## Before all that

My path into tech started well before web dev or AI. In school I was assembling 3D printers, building Arduino robots and a 3D-printed [CubeSat](/projects/cubesat/), replicating a home [light interferometer](/timeline/ligo/), and levitating bits of styrofoam with ultrasonic standing waves.

Since 2017 I've taken part in 50+ hackathons across Ethereum, Solana, Arweave, embedded hardware, and game jams, picking up more than a dozen prizes along the way. One hackathon build ([DumDumDeploy](/projects/dumdumdeploy/)) grew into [ArLink](https://arlink.arweave.net/), Arweave's flagship deployment tool, which raised a seed round at a $2.5M valuation. In 2023 I won the first Arweave India Hacker House — the story is in [this talk](/talks/arweave-hackerhouse-story/).

Outside of day-to-day startup work, I write homebrew for my [New Nintendo 3DS](/console-collection/new-nintendo-3ds/) (including a native C port of [Silent Hill 2](/projects/sh23ds/)) and share builds, explainers, and experiments on [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@ankushkun).
