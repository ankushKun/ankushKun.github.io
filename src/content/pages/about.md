I'm building [Construct Computer](https://construct.computer/?utm_source=ankush.one&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=about): a workforce of AI employees, each with its own cloud computer (a browser, a terminal, files, email and memory), so it can finish the work instead of only talking about it. We launched on Product Hunt in August 2026 and finished #1 Product of the Day.

## How I got here

It started with hardware. In school I was assembling 3D printers, building Arduino robots and a 3D-printed [CubeSat](/projects/cubesat/), replicating a [light interferometer](/timeline/ligo/) at home, and levitating bits of styrofoam with ultrasonic standing waves.

Since 2017 I've taken part in 50+ hackathons across Ethereum, Solana, Arweave, embedded hardware and game jams, and picked up more than a dozen prizes. One of those builds, [DumDumDeploy](/projects/dumdumdeploy/), grew into [ArLink](https://arlink.arweave.net/), Arweave's flagship deployment tool, which raised a seed round at a $2.5M valuation. In 2023 I won the first Arweave India Hacker House with [BetterIDEa](/projects/betteridea/), a web IDE for Arweave and AO. It debuted at AO's global launch, grew to 1,000+ builders and was acquired by Forward Research, where I joined as Entrepreneur in Residence. The story is in [this talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LWW4BCZPIUg).
