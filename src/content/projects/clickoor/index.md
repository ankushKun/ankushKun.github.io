**Clickoor** was a point-and-shoot camera experiment that never runs out of film. Built as a lightweight mobile web camera interface backed by Arweave, every shot automatically uploads to permanent on-chain storage with zero configuration.

Demoed live alongside BetterIDEa at Arweave India Demo Day.
