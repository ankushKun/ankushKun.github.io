**Clickoor** was a point-and-shoot camera experiment designed around one simple idea: a camera that never runs out of film and never loses a roll.

Every shutter tap from the lightweight mobile web UI uploads the photo straight to **Arweave** permanent storage with zero manual setup. Demoed live alongside [BetterIDEa](/projects/betteridea/) at Arweave India Demo Day.
