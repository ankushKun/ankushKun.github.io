A budget take on 6DOF VR rhythm gaming for regular Android phones.

I built a *Beat Saber* clone in **Unity + C#** using the **Google Cardboard SDK** for stereo VR and **Vuforia** to track printed paper AR markers held in each hand — giving full 6-degrees-of-freedom sabers with practically $0 in extra hardware beyond a phone and cardboard viewer. Somehow it's still up on the Amazon Appstore years later.
