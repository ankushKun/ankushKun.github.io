After beating Red on top of Mt. Silver, the storm gets colder than it has ever been. Your team faints one by one, there's no Escape Rope left in your bag, and you pass out in the snow. You wake up in a strange realm with twisted rules where you aren't fighting for badges anymore — you fight to survive, and every win pulls you a step closer to escaping.

I built it in Godot for the GMTK Game Jam 2025 around the theme **Loop**, turning the whole game into an endless gauntlet of battles. I submitted on August 1 and pushed builds through August 3 (V1, V3, and V4 are up on GitHub Releases). It ended up disqualified from official judging for using Pokémon sprites I didn't hold rights to — fair enough, still a fun build.
