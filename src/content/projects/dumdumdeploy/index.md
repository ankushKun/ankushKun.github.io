**DumDumDeploy** was a Vercel-style deployment platform for publishing web apps straight onto the Arweave permaweb — connect a repo, build, and deploy without wrangling wallets or upload scripts manually.

I built the original DumDumDeploy platform from the ground up for **Weavers Raid III**, where it took third place. After the hackathon it outgrew the MVP and rebranded into **[ArLink](https://arlink.arweave.net/)**, now Arweave's flagship deployment platform developed by ArLink Labs (which went on to raise a seed round at a $2.5M valuation).
