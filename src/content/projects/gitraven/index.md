**GitRaven** turns open GitHub issues into funded bounties: maintainers lock rewards in a smart-contract escrow, and contributors get paid automatically when their pull request is merged.

It won 2nd place at [Hack-O-Octo](/timeline/hack-o-octo/) and picked up a Tableland prize pool at [ETHOnline 2023](/timeline/ethg23/) after I rebuilt its indexing layer on Tableland.
