**Weeblet Store** was my attempt at shipping a real indie merch shop from scratch, handling product catalogs, drops, and content edits without wrestling with bloated storefront templates.

## How it was built

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS for fast product browsing and mobile checkout flows
- **Catalog CMS:** Sanity CMS so new merch drops, variants, and inventory could be updated without redeploying the site

The shop is archived now, though an older snapshot is still preserved on the Wayback Machine alongside the full source on GitHub.
