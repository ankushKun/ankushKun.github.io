A campus utility built in **React Native**, **Expo**, and **Firebase** that mapped vacant classrooms in real time so students could find quiet study rooms or spaces for club meetups without barging into lectures.

It picked up over 1,000 downloads on the Google Play Store before we took it down after running into friction with university administration.
