Frustrated by every decent iOS photo editor locking curves and HSL sliders behind monthly subscriptions, I built my own over a single 48-hour weekend and shipped it to the App Store completely free.

## Features

- Curves, HSL adjustments, selective color grading, and filters
- GPU-accelerated processing through **CoreImage**, running 100% on-device with no uploads
- Open source, built in **Swift** and **SwiftUI**
