**You are the last survivor of your kind. Or so you thought.**

I built *Last Cat Standing* during the Arweave India Stealth House in Pondicherry and shipped it to itch.io in September 2025. It's made in Unity and runs directly in the browser via WebGL.

## Gameplay

- **Survive:** You're hungry and terrified of water, living off the apples scattered along the trail.
- **Climb:** Navigate obstacles on the way to the top of the cliff, hoping there's fish waiting up there.
- **Race:** Aim for the fastest run or collect as many apples as you can on the climb.
