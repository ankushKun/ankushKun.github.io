Custom drone flight-controller firmware built for C-DAC's indigenous **RISC-V VEGA Aries V2** microcontroller board, featuring sensor calibration, attitude estimation from an IMU, and multi-axis PID stabilization loops.

Built for **C-DAC's GCON Vegathon** — shortlisted to the top 6 teams for the proof-of-concept round and went on to win **1st prize** at the finals hosted at IIT Guwahati's IEEE GCON conference.
