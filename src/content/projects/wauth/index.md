**WAuth** lets users sign into Arweave web apps with Google, X, GitHub, or Discord — removing wallet extensions and seed phrases from the onboarding flow.

Most apps lose visitors at the "Connect Wallet" screen. WAuth generates and manages a non-custodial wallet quietly in the background using OIT authentication and exposes a drop-in React/Next.js SDK (`@wauth/sdk`) so teams can onboard everyday users in a few lines of code.

Built while I was an Entrepreneur in Residence at Forward Research, `@wauth/sdk` crossed ~5,300 npm downloads and was later adopted by ArLink Labs.
