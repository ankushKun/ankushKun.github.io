**Desonity** packaged the **DeSo (Decentralized Social)** blockchain into drop-in C# components for Unity so indie game devs could add web3 identity and items without writing raw HTTP/crypto glue from scratch.

## What it shipped with

- Wallet sign-in, NFT inventory lookups, tipping, and in-game social posts as reusable Unity prefabs
- Full documentation site and runnable demo scenes (including the [Unity DeSo NFT Item Marketplace](/projects/deso-marketplace/))

Used by several early games experimenting across the DeSo ecosystem.
