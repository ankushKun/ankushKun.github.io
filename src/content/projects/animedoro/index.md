A free browser timer for the **Animedoro** study method — 40 to 60 minutes of focused work followed by one anime episode (~20 minutes) as a self-contained break.

Built in pure vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript back when I was first learning web development, with no frameworks and no account required. Five years later it's still my most-starred repository and my most-visited page.

For the full breakdown of how the ratio works and how it compares to Pomodoro, read [The Animedoro Technique](/blogs/animedoro-technique/).
