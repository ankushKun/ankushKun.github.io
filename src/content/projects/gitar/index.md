**GitAR** adapted the idea behind [GitRaven](/projects/gitraven/) specifically for the Arweave ecosystem: repository maintainers attach crypto bounties to GitHub issues, and contributors claim payouts automatically once their pull requests merge.

Shipping GitAR earned me an [Arweave Scholar](/timeline/ar-scholar/) spot and a sponsored ticket to Arweave India's first Bangalore hackerhouse — which led directly to BetterIDEa.
