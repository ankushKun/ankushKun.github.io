Built for the **2019 Tinkerfest Junior Robotics Competition**: an autonomous LEGO + Arduino + Android robot car designed to sort colored boxes into separate bins.

Venue lighting completely threw off our color detection on competition day, so the bot did not behave as expected — still a blast to build, and it directly led to getting vision tracking working properly on the [Human Following Robot](/projects/human-following/) a few months later.
