A budget 1U CubeSat prototype built from scratch to learn satellite subsystems and telemetry encoding hands-on.

- **Frame:** Custom 3D-printed chassis built to standard 1U dimensions
- **Comms:** Continuous-Wave (CW) Morse telemetry transmitted over a 433 MHz RF transmitter/receiver pair between two Arduinos
