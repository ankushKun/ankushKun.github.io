Ahrefs moves Domain Rating to DR4 on December 1, and when I checked 100 sites, 88 of them scored lower. Our own site goes from 41 to 11. So we built a checker at [Construct](/projects/construct-computer/) that puts DR4 next to DR3 for up to five sites at once, with each site's Tranco rank, age and Wikipedia links, and launched it on Product Hunt.

The whole study is on [construct.computer](https://construct.computer/blog/ahrefs-dr4/).
