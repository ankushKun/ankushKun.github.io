Started in April 2023 when C-DAC accepted our proposal and shipped us an indigenous **VEGA Aries V2** RISC-V microcontroller board. By May we made the top 6 proof-of-concept shortlist and went all-in building a [quadcopter flight controller](/projects/vega-flight-controller/) from scratch with IMU calibration and PID stabilization.

In June we flew out to **IIT Guwahati** for the IEEE GCON conference finals and took **1st prize**.
