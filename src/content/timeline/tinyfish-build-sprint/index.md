Construct was accepted into the Build Sprint of the [TinyFish Accelerator](https://www.tinyfish.ai/accelerator), a two-week virtual intensive from March 31 to April 14 for founders building AI agents that operate on the live web.

It lined up right with our 10-day sprint migrating Construct's backend off a single VPS onto Cloudflare Workers, Durable Objects, and Containers.
