Less than a month after Construct's launch I was back on Product Hunt, this time with [NepoNet](/projects/neponet/). Every intro that helped Construct's launch came through a friend of a friend, and finding those chains by hand took weeks, so we built a tool to automate it.

Vibhansh Alok designed it and I built it. The remote MCP server and bring-your-own-AI plans landed in the days before launch, and the day after, the API moved onto our own server because the follow graph outgrew its Durable Object. It finished #9 Product of the Day. If you're planning a launch of your own, here is [how to promote a Product Hunt launch](/blogs/where-to-promote-product-hunt-launch/).
