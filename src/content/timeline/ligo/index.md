Replicated the optical physics behind **LIGO** (Laser Interferometer Gravitational-Wave Observatory) on my desk at home.

- **Build:** Microscope slides as a beam splitter, broken mirror shards, a cheap laser pointer, and a salvaged magnifying lens.
- **Result:** Projected clean light interference fringes onto the wall, making wave interference visible with junk-drawer optics.
